Dystrophin normally links the intracellular actin cytoskeleton to the extracellular matrix through the sarcolemma, stabilizing the muscle fiber membrane during repeated contraction-relaxation cycles. Without dystrophin, the sarcolemma becomes mechanically unstable, causing progressive membrane damage with each contraction.
